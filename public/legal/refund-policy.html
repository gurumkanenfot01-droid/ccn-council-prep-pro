Refund Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Subscriptions to CCN Council Prep Pro give immediate access to digital study content. Because of this, payments are generally not refundable once access has been given, except in the cases below.

You will get a full refund if

  • You were charged more than once for the same purchase (duplicate payment).
  • Your payment was successful but your subscription was not activated, and we cannot activate it within 48 hours of you contacting us.
  • A technical fault on our side stops you from using the paid features, and we cannot fix it within 72 hours of you reporting it.

How to ask for a refund

  1. Email gurumkanenfot01@gmail.com within 7 days of the payment.
  2. Include the email address of your account, the date and amount paid, and the Paystack payment reference (from your receipt or bank alert).
  3. Tell us briefly what went wrong.

What happens next

  • We reply within 2 working days.
  • Approved refunds are sent back to your original payment method through Paystack within 5–10 working days (your bank may take a little longer to show it).
  • When a refund is made, the paid access for that payment is removed.

Not refundable

  • Change of mind after access has been given.
  • Unused time left on a plan.
  • Accounts suspended for breaking our Terms and Conditions (for example, sharing an account or copying content).
