Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information CCN Council Prep Pro and the CCN Exam Room collect, why, and your choices. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. The data controller is Nenfot (Nenfot Gurumka), Plateau State, Nigeria.

Information we collect

  • Account: your email address and password (passwords are stored securely in encrypted form by our database provider; we cannot read them).
  • Study activity: questions answered, scores, bookmarks and progress, so the app can show your results and weak topics.
  • Payments: the plan bought, amount, date and the Paystack payment reference. Card and bank details are handled only by Paystack and never reach us.
  • CCN Exam Room (proctored mock exams): your name and access code; a face photo and face measurement used to confirm it is you; during an exam, camera and microphone checks, small photos taken when an exam rule may have been broken, and your answers. An administrator may watch the camera live during an exam to supervise it; live viewing is not recorded.
  • Technical: device and browser type and basic logs needed to keep the service working and secure.

How we use it

  • To provide the service, your subscription and your results.
  • To run fair, supervised mock exams and prevent cheating.
  • To answer your messages and send important notices about your account.
  • To fix problems and improve the content.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. We share it only with services that help us run the app, for these purposes only: Supabase (database and login), Vercel (website hosting) and Paystack (payments); or when required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep account and study data while your account is active. Exam Room photos and monitoring records are kept only as long as needed to review exam results, and are deleted on request. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how it is used, by emailing gurumkanenfot01@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access is restricted to authorised people only.

Children

The service is meant for nurses and nursing students aged 18 and over.
